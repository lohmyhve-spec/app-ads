RainJar Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

RainJar ("the app") is published by Meongmungi (Mung Works). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

1. Information the app uses

  • Field locations you enter (name, latitude and longitude). Names and coordinates are stored on your device. To show rainfall, the app sends the coordinates (not the name) to our rain data server.
  • Gauge readings you enter, unit setting and Pro status. Stored only on your device.
  • Advertising. Free users see banner ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID and usage data to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Purchases. Pro purchases are handled by Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone). We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Your device location, only when you tap "Use my location" (first start or when adding a field). It is used once to set that field's coordinates and is handled like the field location above. The app does not track your location in the background.

The app does not ask for an account and does not read your contacts, photos or files.

2. Rain data server

Our server receives the coordinates and the date range, looks up rainfall in public NOAA MRMS data, and returns the hourly amounts. Requests travel over HTTPS. Server logs may contain the coordinates and request time and are deleted within 30 days. Coordinates are not linked to any name, account or advertising ID on our side.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with Google as described above (ads, purchases, app stores) and with our cloud hosting provider that runs the rain data server.

4. Your choices and deletion

  • Delete a field in the app (Fields tab) to remove it and its gauge readings from your device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Pro users see no ads.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

6. Data source

Rainfall estimates come from NOAA MRMS public data. NOAA does not endorse this app.

7. Changes

We will post updates to this page and change the effective date.
