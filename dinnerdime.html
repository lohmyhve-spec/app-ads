DinnerDime Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026
App: DinnerDime (Android package com.meongmoong.dinnerdime, iOS)
Developer: Meongmungi (Mung Works)

DinnerDime helps you plan a week of dinners, build a shopping list, and estimate what it will cost. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. The short version: we do not run accounts or a server for your data. Your plans, settings and receipt totals stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising and app store services described below collect.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We (the developer) never receive it.

  • Your settings: ZIP code, preferred grocery chain, household size, cooking days, weekly budget, and the food preferences you choose (ingredients to skip, allergens to avoid, diet and cuisine choices, kitchen equipment you don't have).
  • Your weekly dinner plans, shopping lists, and items you mark as "have at home".
  • Receipt records you add: the store, the date, and the total amount.
  • Ad and purchase state (for example, when an ad was last shown and whether you bought ad removal).

Your ZIP code is used on your device only, to pick regional price estimates. It is not sent to us.

2. Receipt scanning (camera)

If you choose to scan a receipt, the app asks for camera access and takes one photo. The text on the receipt is read on your device by Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs offline inside the app. The receipt image is not uploaded to us or to Google, and the app deletes the image after reading it. Only the store, date and total you confirm are saved, on your device. You can skip scanning and type the total yourself.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or, if you allow tracking on iOS, the IDFA
  • Device and operating system information, app version, and crash diagnostics
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions (which ads were shown and tapped)

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, and certain U.S. states), the app shows Google's consent message before personalized ads. You can change your choice later from the "Privacy options" button in the app.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google (or Privacy) > Ads.
  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the IDFA is not used. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Buying ad removal stops all ads in the app.

4. In-app purchase (remove ads)

Ad removal is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your name, email, or payment card. The app only receives a confirmation that the purchase belongs to this device's store account, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase later.

6. Sharing and "sale" of personal information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone ourselves, because it never reaches us. The advertising data in section 3 is collected directly by Google. Under some U.S. state privacy laws, use of advertising identifiers for personalized ads can be treated as "sharing" for targeted advertising; you can opt out using the choices in section 3 or by buying ad removal.

7. Children

DinnerDime is meant for adults who plan household meals. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data security, retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it. You can delete individual plans and receipts in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Because we have no account system or server copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To ask Google about advertising data, use the links in section 3.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app starts using new services (for example, a server for live grocery prices), we will update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above.
