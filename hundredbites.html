What 100 Bites is

100 Bites helps a parent or caregiver record the foods a baby has tried and track how the 9 common allergens are introduced and kept in the diet. It is a record-keeping tool only. It does not diagnose allergies or give medical advice. Talk to your pediatrician before introducing allergens, and call emergency services if your child shows signs of a severe reaction.

Data you enter

The baby's nickname, birth date, food records, reactions, notes and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a server for this data, there is no account, and we do not receive, sell or share it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. The backup file and the pediatrician PDF report are created only when you choose, and you decide where they are saved or sent.

Notifications and widget

If you turn on reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The home screen widget reads your records on the device. Nothing is sent to us.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location, ad interaction data and diagnostics to show and measure ads. Where required, we ask for your consent (Google UMP, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency on iOS). You can change your choice in Settings > Privacy options and limit tracking in your device settings. See Google's advertising policy. We do not send the records you enter to advertisers.

Purchases

A one-time purchase removes ads. Payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we do not receive your payment details. You can restore the purchase from Settings.

Children

100 Bites is intended for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will be posted on this page.
